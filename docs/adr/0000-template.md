# NNNN. Short title in the imperative

**Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What situation forced a decision? What constraints applied? Write enough that
someone with no memory of this period understands the problem.

## Decision

What we decided, stated plainly.

## Alternatives considered

What else was on the table, and why it lost. **Do not skip this section** — it is
the part that stops the decision being re-litigated later.

## Consequences

What this makes easy, what it makes hard, and what it commits us to. Include the
costs honestly; an ADR listing only benefits is not trustworthy.
